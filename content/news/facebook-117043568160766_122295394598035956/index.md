A new Fosse Falcons tradition begins 👔  
  
A proud moment for our cluster on Sunday as we handed out our first ever Fosse Falcons ties to our U12s&#44; U14s &#38; U16s  
  
Designed especially for the Fosse Falcons&#44; the tie brings together the colours of all our home clubs and gives our girls something that represents their team &#38; the cluster  
  
From now on&#44; you’ll see our girls swapping their rugby shirts for white shirts and ties after matches&#44; honouring a little bit of rugby tradition along the way   
  
Seeing so many of them wearing them together for the first time was pretty special 🥹  
  
And for any other girls who haven’t received theirs yet… don’t worry&#44; the next batch is already on its way&#33;
