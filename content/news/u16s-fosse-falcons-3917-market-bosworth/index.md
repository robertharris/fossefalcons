Another fantastic performance from our U16s as they welcomed Market Bosworth to Old Leamingtonians on Sunday for a friendly ahead of their league meeting later this season  
  
The Falcons came out firing&#44; with forwards and backs linking brilliantly to build an early lead with three unanswered tries  
  
Powerful running from Georgia&#44; Eliza and Rosie created plenty of opportunities&#44; with Katie directing play and making great use of the pace out wide  
  
Market Bosworth fought back strongly&#44; turning the game into a tough&#44; physical contest and making the Falcons work hard in defence  
  
Try scorers&#58;  
Eliza ×3&#44; Bethan&#44; Georgia&#44; Katie H &#38; Katie J  
  
Conversions&#58; Charlotte B ×2  
  
Players’ Player&#58; Eliza – a brilliant hat&#45;trick&#33;  
Coaches’ Player&#58; Katie H  
  
Special mention to Katie J for getting involved in more rucks than any full&#45;back before her&#33; 😂  
  
A great team effort&#44; plenty of positives to take away and lots to build on before the league encounter&#33;  
  
Thanks to Market Bosworth for a competitive and enjoyable game&#33;
