## Your choice

Google Analytics is optional. When it is enabled on this website, we ask before loading it or setting its analytics cookies. Accept and Reject are equally available, and you can use the whole site without accepting.

If analytics is disabled in the site's configuration, no Google Analytics scripts load and no analytics consent prompt is shown.

## What accepting allows

If enabled and accepted, Google Analytics 4 sends information about your visits to Google, including page views, browser and device information, and technical network information needed to deliver the service. Google processes this information to provide website usage reports.

The site does not enable Google Signals or advertising personalisation in its analytics integration. It excludes query strings and fragments from the page address and referral address sent with page views. We do not intentionally send player details, names or email addresses to Analytics.

Google Analytics can create first-party cookies named `_ga` and `_ga_…` to distinguish browsers and sessions. Our integration sets a maximum cookie lifetime of 180 days without extending it on each visit. See [Google's explanation of how it uses information from other sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Remembering your decision

When analytics is enabled, we store your Accept or Reject choice in your browser's local storage for 180 days. This preference is used only to remember your decision, not to track you. After it expires, we ask again. Clearing browser storage also removes the preference.

If your browser prevents us from reading or saving your preference, analytics stays off for that page and we display a message explaining the problem.

## Analytics settings

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer to see your current choice and change it at any time. Rejecting after previously accepting stops further collection, removes accessible Google Analytics cookies for this site and reloads the page to unload Analytics. It does not erase information already sent to Google.

Without JavaScript, the consent controls and Google Analytics do not run.

These controls apply to Google Analytics only. Links to other services, such as Spond and social media, are governed by those services' privacy information. Any embedded third-party content needs its own privacy review.
