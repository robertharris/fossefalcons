## When and where

Training takes place on Wednesday evenings with fixtures and traning on Sundays. Training rotates around our clubs. We use the [Spond App](https://spond.com/landing/login/) to organise ourselves for training and match availability.


## What to bring

- Gum shield
- Water bottle
- Boots (studs suitable for grass)
- Warm and waterproof layers

All sessions are led by qualified, DBS-checked coaches.
