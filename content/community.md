## Making a difference in our local community

At Fosse Falcons, rugby is about more than what happens on the pitch. We’re proud to encourage our girls to give back to their local communities through volunteering, fundraising and supporting local causes.

## Supporting Stratford-upon-Avon Foodbank

Over two Harvest collections, our players, parents and supporters have generously donated food and essential supplies to Stratford-upon-Avon Foodbank, helping local families and individuals in need.

![Harvest collection of food and supplies for Stratford-upon-Avon Foodbank](/images/community/community4.jpeg)
![Basket and box of donated food and toiletries with a hand-drawn Food Bank Collection sign](/images/community/community3.jpeg)

## Looking After Our Environment

Our Falcons have also been getting involved in community litter picks, working together to keep our local spaces clean and welcoming for everyone.

![Falcons players and supporters with litter pickers and bags at a community litter pick](/images/community/community2.jpeg)
![Volunteer wearing a Rubbish Friends Stratford upon Avon hi-vis vest](/images/community/community1.jpeg)

## Can Our Falcons Help?

We’re always looking for new ways to support our local communities. If you’re a charity, organisation or community group in Stratford-upon-Avon, Shipston-on-Stour, Rugby or the surrounding areas and could use a helping hand, we’d love to hear from you.

Get in touch and let’s see how our Falcons can help! Email [info@fossefalcons.co.uk](mailto:info@fossefalcons.co.uk).
