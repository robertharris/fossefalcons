We're delighted that Charlie is coaching our U12 team this year.

- Tell us a little about yourself and your rugby coaching background

My 18-year-old daughter started playing rugby when she was around 13. I spent a lot of time sitting in the car, getting cold and bored, so I decided to get involved on the pitch instead! Five years later, she's gone off to university, but I'm still coaching!

- What made you want to get involved with Fosse Falcons?

I've been involved with Fosse for quite a while, and the culture here is fantastic. The girls have so much fun, and bringing clubs together as a cluster means we can pool our players, coaches and resources.
It gives the girls more game time, better opportunities to develop their skills and access to a great mix of coaching experience. It's what makes Fosse such a great group

- What would you say to a girl who's nervous about trying rugby?

You can go as fast or as slowly as you like! If you're keen to get stuck into tackling, brilliant. But if you're not quite ready, there's absolutely no pressure.
We'll support you at your own pace, help you build confidence and, most importantly, make sure you enjoy yourself.

- Finally, who's your favourite rugby team and player?

Leicester Tigers are my favourite team. As for players, I'd have to say Abi Burton and Delaney Burns. They're both fantastic players, and I've been lucky enough to meet them. They're lovely people too!
