We’re excited to have Becky coaching our U18s this season.

Becky brings over 20 years of rugby experience and a passion for helping players grow in confidence, develop new skills and find their voice on the pitch.

Her coaching style is built around encouragement, teamwork and creating an environment where every player feels valued and supported.

“There’s nothing better than seeing a player master a new skill or find their confidence on the pitch”
