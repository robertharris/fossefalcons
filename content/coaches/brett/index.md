Brett is our U14 head coach.

- What inspired you to become a coach? 

I kind of fell into it!  The original coaches, for my eldest son, wanted some help and asked.  By the end of the season all the old coaches had stepped back and the new coaches stepped in.  Wouldn’t change it for the world! 

- What’s one piece of advice you would give to your players? 

Back yourself!  Work each week to be better.  Don’t sweat the small stuff. 

- Who’s your biggest role model (in or out of sports?) 

Hard not to have a cringy answer! In truth I probably have a few - my wife, Dad and late Mum - each showed me something fundamental I needed to do better.

- What’s one fun fact people don’t know about you? 

That I support Scotland?  Not fun I suppose, but I do make a mean coffee - welcome anytime for a brew!
