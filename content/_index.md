## Welcome to Fosse Falcons

Fosse Falcons is a friendly girls rugby union team where players of all abilities can learn the game, build confidence and make friends for life.

Established in 2021, Fosse Falcons is a cluster of clubs from across Warwickshire. We have over 120 girls playing and training from U12 to U18.

New to rugby? No experience is needed – come along to a session and give it a try.
